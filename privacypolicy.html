DUO DUNGEON · ANDROID HEALTH CONNECT

Health Data Privacy

Duo Dungeon reads your step count from Android Health Connect only after you choose to connect and grant permission.

What we access

The Android app requests read access to Steps only (READ_STEPS). It does not request permission to read heart rate, sleep, location, workouts, calories, or other health categories. It does not write health records.

How syncing works

When you tap Sync, Duo Dungeon reads an aggregated total for the current local day. It does not upload individual health records, timestamps for individual steps, or source-device history. The app sends the aggregate step total, the local calendar date, and your device time-zone identifier to the authenticated Duo Dungeon step-reward service so the server can validate and calculate game rewards.

Rewards and storage

The server remains the authority for step rewards, daily limits, duplicate protection, and save revisions. The Android app does not grant coins, experience, or other rewards locally. Health Connect data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Your choice and controls

Sync is initiated by you from inside Duo Dungeon. No background step syncing is promised or performed by this integration. You can deny or revoke Steps access at any time in Android Health Connect settings. If permission is denied or revoked, syncing stops until you choose to grant it again.

Questions

For account or game-save questions, use the Account section in Duo Dungeon. For Health Connect permission questions, review the permissions shown by Android on your device.

This notice describes the Android Health Connect step-sync feature. It does not replace the general Duo Dungeon privacy notice.
