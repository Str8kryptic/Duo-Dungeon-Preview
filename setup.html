WELCOME, CRAWLER

Your steps are your dungeon fuel.

Your Duo Dungeon account is ready. Install the official Apple Shortcut below to send today's step total into your game. No hand-building actions or editing URL placeholders required.

Use the same iPhone where Apple Health records your walking. The Shortcut requests access to your Steps data and sends today's total to Duo Dungeon; it does not give the game direct access to your Health records.

INSTALL SHORTCUT ↗
FIRST-TIME SETUP · THREE STEPS

Install. Connect. Sync.

Already installed the Shortcut? Skip Step 1 and go straight to Step 2.

1
Install the Shortcut

Tap Add Official Apple Health Shortcut above. When Apple Shortcuts opens, review it and tap Add Shortcut. Keep its name as Sync Duo Dungeon – DEV.

2
Connect your crawler account

Return to Duo Dungeon in Safari and sign in to the account that should receive your steps. When Apple asks for Health access, allow the Shortcut to read Steps. If permission is not requested until the first run, approve it then.

3
Run your first sync

Open the Apple Shortcuts app, find Sync Duo Dungeon – DEV, and tap its play button. If asked, allow access to read Apple Health Steps. When Safari opens Duo Dungeon, sign in if needed, check the correct crawler and today's total, then tap Confirm Step Sync. Wait for the success message.

IMPORTANT · ACCOUNT & REWARDS

Where do my steps go?

  • Steps are credited to the Duo Dungeon account currently signed in when the Shortcut opens the game.
  • Always confirm the correct crawler before applying a reading, especially on a shared device.
  • The Shortcut sends today's cumulative total. Duo Dungeon compares it with your last successful sync and rewards only the new increase, so running it twice does not double-count steps.
  • Keep the Shortcut installed; run it whenever you want to sync your latest steps.

If the game opens signed out, sign in first, then run the Shortcut again. Do not clear Safari website data or delete your save to troubleshoot a sync.
